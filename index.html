Questions

How does it stay hidden?

Stealth Panel uses a Windows feature (capture exclusion) that keeps a window out of screen captures. Apps that capture your screen through Windows see what's behind the panel, not the panel itself. That includes Zoom, Meet in the browser, Teams, OBS, Loom, Discord and the Snipping Tool.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 version 2004 or newer, and Windows 11. A macOS version is on the roadmap.

Can people see my mouse move over it?

If you share your entire screen, viewers can see your cursor. They just don't see the panel it's over. To avoid that, share a single window, or use ghost mode so your clicks pass through to the app you're presenting.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Notes, snippets and settings stay on your PC. Voice-following runs offline. The optional AI feature sends your question and open documents to Anthropic only when you ask, using your own API key.

What does "one-time" mean?

You pay once and keep Pro forever on up to 2 PCs, including future updates to version 1.x. There's no subscription.

Can I use it in interviews or exams?

Stealth Panel is built for presenting and recording your own content. Follow the rules of whatever call, class or assessment you're in. We don't support using it to break them.
