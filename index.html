Questions

How does it stay hidden?

Windows has a setting, called capture exclusion, that keeps a window out of screen captures. Stealth Panel turns it on. Apps that capture your screen through Windows see what's behind the panel, not the panel itself. That includes Zoom, Meet in the browser, Teams, OBS, Loom, Discord and the Snipping Tool.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and Windows 11. A macOS version is planned.

Can people see my mouse move over it?

If you share your entire screen, viewers see your cursor but not the panel under it. To avoid that, share a single window, or turn on ghost mode so clicks pass through to the app you're presenting.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Notes, snippets and settings stay on your PC, and voice-following runs offline. The optional AI feature sends your question and open documents to Anthropic only when you ask, using your own API key.

How does the subscription work?

Pro costs $10.99 a month or $79 a year and works on up to 2 PCs, with all updates included. Cancel anytime from the link in your receipt email. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then the app goes back to the free version. Your notes stay on your PC either way.

Can I use it in interviews or exams?

Stealth Panel is built for presenting and recording your own content. Follow the rules of whatever call, class or assessment you're in. We don't support using it to break them.
