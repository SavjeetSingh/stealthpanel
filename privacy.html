Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Short version: your notes, files, snippets and settings stay on your PC. We don't have accounts, analytics or ads. The app only contacts the internet to check your license, look for updates, load the web pages you open, and, if you choose to use it, send questions to the AI provider.

This policy covers the Stealth Panel desktop app for Windows (the "App") and this website (the "Site"). "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of Stealth Panel.

1. Data that stays on your device

Everything you create or open in the App is stored only on your computer, in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\stealth-panel). That includes notes, snippets, open tabs, settings, window position, your license key and your optional AI API key. We never receive it. Your license key and API key are encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection.

2. Data sent over the internet

  • License checks (Pro only). When you activate Pro, and then about every 12 hours, the App sends your license key, an activation ID and your computer's name to Dodo Payments to confirm the license is valid. Your computer's name is used to label the activation, so you can tell your PCs apart.
  • Update checks. The App checks GitHub Releases for new versions and downloads updates from GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Web tabs. Websites you open in the App's browser tabs receive the same information any browser would send them. Their cookies are stored locally in the App.
  • Speech engine download (Meeting listening, Pro). The first time you turn on Meeting listening, the App downloads the open-source whisper.cpp speech engine from GitHub and its English model from Hugging Face, and checks both against fixed checksums before using them. Like any download, GitHub and Hugging Face receive your IP address. After that, no network is needed to transcribe.
  • Ask AI (optional, Pro). The AI feature is only used when you ask a question. Your question, plus the text of your open notes, files, slides and the web page you're viewing — including a meeting transcript, if one is open — is sent directly from your PC to Anthropic using your own API key. We never see it. Anthropic's privacy policy and your agreement with Anthropic apply.

3. Meeting listening (optional, Pro)

  • What it hears. While Meeting listening is on, the App captures the sound your PC plays and the sound from your microphone. It hears everything your PC plays, not only the call you mean — other meetings, videos, music.
  • Where it's processed. Speech is turned into text by whisper.cpp running on your PC. Audio is held in memory for a few seconds at a time while it's transcribed, then discarded. It is never saved to disk and never uploaded.
  • What's kept. The transcript is saved as a note on your PC, like any other note, until you delete it.
  • Answers. If you press Answer, or turn on Auto, the question and your open notes — including the transcript — are sent to your chosen AI provider, exactly as described under Ask AI above. If you don't, the transcript never leaves your PC.
  • Other people. The people you're talking to are not told by the App that it's listening. In many places the law requires their consent before a conversation is recorded or transcribed; getting it is your responsibility.
  • It is off by default, and stops the moment you stop it or close the App.

4. Voice-following teleprompter

Speech recognition uses the recognizer built into Windows and runs entirely on your PC. Audio is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere by the App.

5. Purchases

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Dodo collects your name, email, billing details and payment information to process the order, calculate tax and send your license key. We receive order details such as your name, email, country and purchase history so we can provide support and handle refunds. We never receive your full card details. See Dodo's privacy policy.

6. This website

The Site is hosted on Vercel, which may log basic request data such as IP address and browser type for security and to operate the service. The Site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

7. Support emails

If you email us, we use your email and what you send only to help you, and we keep it only as long as needed to do that.

8. Your choices and rights

  • To delete everything the App stored, uninstall it and delete the %APPDATA%\stealth-panel folder.
  • You can deactivate your license on a PC at any time from Settings → License.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the purchase data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Some records must be kept for tax and legal reasons.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the release notes.
